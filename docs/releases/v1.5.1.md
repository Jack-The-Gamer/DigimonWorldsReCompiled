Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.5.1** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.5.0
Fixes for widescreen in Digimon World:
- **Scene fades cover the whole picture.** When the screen faded to black (leaving an area, entering a building), the extra parts at the sides stayed bright. They now fade with the rest.
- **The field stops at the end of the map.** Walking to the end of an area scrolled the picture on until black showed at the side. The view now stops where the end of the map meets the edge of the wider picture. This is the one place widescreen changes anything in the game itself: Digimon World's camera stops a little earlier at the ends of a map while widescreen is on. Nothing else about how it plays changes.
- **Battle start.** The black edges of the "BATTLE" banner no longer fly out to the sides of the screen, and with Widescreen UI the battle HUD appears in place instead of in pieces that snap together a moment later.
- **The HUD comes back in place.** With Widescreen UI, the clock and the status bars no longer appear in the middle for a moment after a battle or after loading a save state.
- **Continue Slot.** With Widescreen UI, the highlighted save slot no longer slides out of its frame on the Continue screen.
