Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.8.3** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.8.2
Widescreen fixes for Digimon World and Digimon World 2003:
- **Digimon World's menus stay together with Widescreen UI.** In battle, the item list's first rows and its cursor slid off to the left with the command icons. In shops and the item bank, the item icons, the "MENU", "PRICE" and "COUNT" labels, the scroll bars and the lines in the amount box stayed behind when their windows moved to the sides. A menu that just opened no longer jumps to the edge for a moment before settling.
- **Items lying on the ground stay on their shadows.** With Widescreen UI, an item near the edge of the screen was pushed out with the HUD and only came back to its place when you walked up to it.
- **Digimon World's camera no longer gets stuck in widescreen.** After training in the Green Gym the screen could stop scrolling up and down, and the game could hang right after "Agumon joins the city!". Widescreen also works fully on the European and Japanese Digimon World discs now: the fields stop scrolling at the ends of the map and their sides are filled in, as on the USA disc.
- **Digimon World 2003's full-screen menus stay in place with Widescreen UI.** On the memory card screens (loading and saving), the text no longer sits outside its panel, and the item and equipment screens no longer come apart.
- **No more bare sides on Digimon World 2003's item screens.** Their scrolling tiled background now fills the sides of the wider picture all the time, instead of leaving one side black half the time.
