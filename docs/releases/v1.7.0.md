Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.7.0** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.6.1
A better **CRT filter** (Settings → Video → CRT):
- **Scanlines like a real tube.** Each line of the picture is now drawn as a beam of light that grows wider on bright colours and narrower on dark ones, so dark scenes show clear gaps between the lines and bright parts blend together, as on a television. The picture's brightness is kept: turning the scanlines up no longer makes the game darker, and bright colours keep their colour instead of washing out.
- **Full detail in Digimon World 2 and 2003 menus and movies.** These use a sharper 480-line picture; the filter used to merge its lines in pairs and lose half the detail. Now every line is shown, as on a television.
- **A phosphor mask that fits your screen.** The red, green and blue stripes are sized to the picture on your screen (finer on a small screen, wider in 4K), so they no longer turn into a visible grid.
- **New Glow slider.** A little light spreads softly through the glass around bright parts of the picture, like text and logos.
- **Smoother edges.** With screen curvature on, the corners are rounded and the edge of the screen is smooth instead of jagged, and a small window no longer shows uneven lines.

Your CRT settings are kept; Glow starts at its default.
