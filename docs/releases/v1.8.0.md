Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.8.0** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.7.0
**Play with the mouse** in Digimon World 1 and Digimon World 2003 (**Settings → Controls → Mouse**, on by default). Click what you want on the picture and Re:Compiled presses the buttons you would press on the pad, so the game plays exactly as it does with a controller. Your keyboard and gamepad keep working alongside it.
- **Left click** picks what you click: a menu option, tab or item, a dialogue choice, something in a shop, a battle command, technique or item, a memory card slot, or New Game / Continue on the title screen. In the field and in dialogue it talks, examines and moves to the next line.
- **Right click** goes back or closes a menu. In the field it opens the menu: Digimon World 1's △ menu, Digimon World 2003's field menu.
- **Digimon World 1 battles** (also tournaments and Vs battles): click a command icon, or the **FINISH!!** sign once the finisher is ready. While a finisher charges, every click counts as an L1/R1 press. Right click ends training early.
- Also clickable in Digimon World 1: the Player menu's evolution chart, medals and cards, the Tech tab's grid, and Vs mode's mode and arena choices. In Digimon World 2003: the party panels, the Digimon Lab, the save and load screens, and the European disc's language screen.
- With **Widescreen UI** on, clicks reach the menus that were moved out to the edges.
- Not yet: Digimon World 2, the card battles in Digimon World 2003 (a click there is only ✕ or △), and picking Digimon in Digimon World 1's Vs mode.

**Every release is now a verified dump.** Digimon World (Europe and Japan), Digimon World 2 (Japan) and Digimon World 3 (USA and Japan) were each played to a new in-game save and back, matched against their Redump dumps, and no longer show "Unverified dump". If you imported one of them before, it is marked verified the next time the launcher starts; there is no need to import it again.
