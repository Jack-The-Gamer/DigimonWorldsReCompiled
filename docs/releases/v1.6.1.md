Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.6.1** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.6.0
Fixes for widescreen in Digimon World:
- **No more black bars on the field.** At some camera positions a black strip showed at the left side of the field. The game keeps only part of the map ready to draw at a time, too little for a wide picture; the sides are now filled from the rest of the map, in battles too. The game itself plays exactly as before.
- **Menu tabs stay on their tabs.** With Widescreen UI, the "Status" label and the "Techset" heading in the Digimon menu were drawn out at the left edge of the screen instead of on their tab and window.
- **No more jumping text when switching pages.** With Widescreen UI, text on a new menu page (like "Spit Fire" on the Tech page) no longer appears in one place and jumps to another a moment later, and a talk box no longer comes up in two pieces.
