Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 3 / 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**Already playing?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update to v1.8.1** at the bottom left of the Games page (or open **Settings → About** and click **Check now**). It downloads the new version and replaces the program where it is; your games, saves, settings and mods are kept.

**New to Re:Compiled?** Pick the zip for your system below, unzip it anywhere, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`). Then click **Scan a folder for discs…** and choose the folder with your disc images.

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 1.8.0
**Mouse clicks in Digimon World 2003's card battles.** Until now a click there was only ✕ or △.
- **Choose a folder** by clicking it.
- **Play a card** by clicking it in your hand: the highlight moves onto it and it is played. The coin toss for who goes first works the same way.
- **Picking a card from your deck**, where the cards are laid out overlapping: the first click highlights the card so you can see it whole, and a second click on it takes it.
- Questions and the card battle menu take clicks too, and **right click answers No**.

Also in Digimon World 2003:
- **Right click answers No** on the story's yes/no questions, which ignore △.
- **Save and load screens:** the first click picks a save and a second click on it loads or saves there, so a stray click no longer loads a save straight away.
