Digimon World, Digimon World 2 and Digimon World 2003 running natively on Windows, Linux and macOS. You need your own disc image (`.bin`; a `.cue` is optional); a BIOS dump is optional.

### Download
**On 0.2.5 or later?** The launcher offers this update when it starts: click **Update** at the bottom left. **On 0.2.2 to 0.2.4?** Open **Settings → About**, click **Check now**, then **Update…**. Otherwise pick the zip for your system below, unzip it, and run `DigimonWorldsReCompiled` (Windows: `DigimonWorldsReCompiled.exe`).

| | |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS, Apple Silicon | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS, Intel | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-macos-x64.zip` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `DigimonWorldsReCompiled-linux-x64.zip` |

The builds are not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm ("More info → Run anyway"), and on macOS right-click the program and choose **Open** the first time.

### Changes since 0.2.8
- **No more "More" button on mods.** Its actions are now buttons on the open card instead: **Version History** and **Open Folder** sit to the left of **Delete** at the bottom of the card, and **Reset options** sits at the top of the mod's options (greyed out while every option is at its default). Version History is the old **Versions…**: every release of the mod, to update or go back to an older one. *Move to top* and *Move to bottom* went with the menu; **Up** and **Down** still change the load order.
